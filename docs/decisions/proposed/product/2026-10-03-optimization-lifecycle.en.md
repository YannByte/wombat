# Decision Note: complete optimization lifecycle and runtime evidence

[中文](2026-10-03-optimization-lifecycle.md) | English

Status: proposed

The current simplification supersedes task sending, alternate installation modes, automatic review history and adoption tracking described here. Other evidence constraints and unfinished items remain. See [read-only checks and one plugin](../../implemented/architecture/2026-10-31-on-demand-checks.en.md).

## Problem

Static configuration facts and focused UI tests do not establish a complete optimization lifecycle. Runtime events, continuous coverage, reliable historical versions, and complete platform journeys remain incomplete. Unknowns are not absence of problems, and related usage or text reduction is not savings. This proposal owns remaining requirements from the former five-entry specification and status ledger. Consolidation neither reduces agreed scope nor restarts product implementation.

## Proposal

Extend shared Rust facts and generated contracts through both Web and noninteractive entries. The [UI README](../../../development/architecture.en.md), [contract](../../../development/contracts.en.md), and [CLI guide](../../../guides/cli.en.md) own current interfaces, fields, and operations. The [native-handoff decision](../../implemented/architecture/2026-10-03-native-codex-handoff.en.md) owns sending/execution responsibilities without duplicating shipped mechanisms here.

### Runtime observations and content evidence

G01: complete reliable Skill invocation, Hook execution, and MCP prompt events. Distinguish calls, reads, loading, and connections; count independent failures/retries and deduplicate stable event replay without merging independent parent/child activity. Zero requires complete coverage without events. Incomplete coverage yields observed counts or unknown. Existing tool/resource and Skill-observation subsets do not complete this requirement.

G02: obtain MCP text from trustworthy complete definitions with versions and measurement methods, without starting services to obtain them. Missing, truncated, limited, or invalid data remains unknown. The contract alone owns existing AGENTS/Skill thresholds and tokenization fields.

G03: provide an automatically included context inventory with reliable origins, actual content versions, scope, size, and recorded counts. Inventory is not itself a defect and counts/size do not predict savings.

G04: same-request duplicate injection requires request/context identity, compaction generation, historical source-content version, and two actual inclusion positions. Deduplicate replay without mixing parent/child activity, new requests, or compaction generations. Complete static blocks, common directories, current files, and repeated reads are insufficient substitutes. Reverify comparable real contexts after correction.

### Continuous observation and resource candidates

G05: Skill/MCP inactivity requires stable identity, continuous enablement, at least thirty consecutive 24-hour periods of complete observation with inclusive boundaries, and no use across every applicable project/client, including failed attempts. Reliable Skill reads also prevent complete-inactivity findings without counting as invocation. Installation/file dates, imported history, or zero within a filtered window do not establish coverage.

The [extension review decision](../../implemented/product/2026-11-01-extension-review.en.md) owns the tradeoffs for Skill, MCP and Hook review through context footprint and use frequency. It selects objects worth reviewing and proposes scoped adjustments from task needs and user choice without equating absent use records with uselessness. G05 retains the complete-inactivity proof requirement.

G06: an inactivity-based worktree, temporary-file or cache recommendation requires at least fourteen days of complete observation, no activity, completed tasks and no running tasks. Direct current evidence can support review of rebuildable project outputs or explicitly selected task temporary paths without this waiting period; it cannot establish historical inactivity or accepted delivery. The [artifact contract](../../../reference/core.en.md#artifact-observations) owns this delivered read-only check and original-condition recheck. Codex owns selection and authorization; supported Codex objects use the guarded core operation described by the current contract. Main directories, pinned/locked worktrees, user assets and pending consumers remain protected. Worktree recommendations, complete inactivity coverage and recovery acceptance remain unfinished. Recoverable relocation does not mean freed space.

G07: initial consecutive-check parameters require at least five checks in one running task, gaps no greater than two seconds, no new output, and no input or prompt-response requirement. Overall coverage needs at least five analyzable tasks, three matching tasks, two matching dates, and a match within the final seven observation days. Exclude necessary keepalives, interaction, long waits, and evidence gaps. Without an editable location show evidence only. Version parameters and test boundary counterexamples.

### Natural adoption and usage reconciliation

G08: under reliable historical versions, distinguish no observation, unknown versions, confirmed adoption, and recurrence. Do not run extra models to establish benefits. Deduplicate actual optimization-task usage against history by stable measurement identities; never add task totals and related usage twice or replace unknown with zero. Text comparisons require equal scope/method and do not establish causal benefits. Checks remain separate from user decisions; rechecks do not revoke keep/not-applicable choices, and missing/unreadable files do not automatically resolve findings.

### Complete product journeys

G09: complete Windows Hooks, platform installations, and failure journeys. The [release Skill](../../../../.agents/skills/wombat-release/SKILL.md) owns release operations and the [GitHub distribution decision](../../implemented/architecture/2026-10-04-github-release-distribution.en.md) owns durable target rationale; the [local-Web decision](../../implemented/architecture/2026-10-01-local-web.en.md) owns desktop scope/tradeoffs without adding Tauri to this Web/CLI repair round. Continuous cross-project journeys across five pages must retain scope, filters, exact turns, lists, focus, and reading position. Late responses, refreshes, and language changes cannot overwrite new selection. Verify Chinese/English at 320/390/1280/1440px, native-select keyboard use, cancellation/closing, unknown delivery, concurrency, and version changes in actual browsers. Complete account-summary, actual-exhaustion, expired-restriction, and independently degraded section checks without deriving allowance from local usage.

Help covers tokens/amounts, usage counts, text size/estimated tokens, related usage, and common questions. Global help starts collapsed; local explanations are directly accessible and restore focus without requiring internal developer identifiers. Amount precision, date semantics, and query conservation follow current contracts and UI documentation.

The local Codex Skill reuses CLI operations without duplicating pages, accounting, or execution services. Isolated installation runs outside the repository and preserves custom same-name Skills, permissions, and independent user data; queries, rankings, fixed-view drill-down, and findings use independent truth. Natural-language routing, ongoing conversation, authorized edits, and rechecks require human-task acceptance rather than installation tests. Public plugins, persistent conversation workspaces, and continuous monitoring are outside local-installation delivery.

### Evidence-backed usage investigations and reviews

Provide investigation candidates, context trajectories, resource hotspots, and period reviews within Wombat's domain boundaries. The [core reference](../../../reference/core.en.md) owns existing period contributions, task/descendant comparisons, and publication-change summaries. G10–G16 below retain the new optimization goals and complete acceptance requirements. The core reference above owns current implementation and limits; partial implementation does not establish complete acceptance.

G10: provide inspectable candidates that combine existing facts such as high usage, low cache reuse, repeated operations, failures, and input growth. Rust returns bounded candidates with metrics, denominators, baselines, threshold or statistical-method versions, coverage, and evidence routes. Codex interprets them and selects a small number worth inspecting. Keep rule matches separate from interpretation and retain conflicting signals and gaps. Partial evidence can support inspection advice. Codex can provide clearly labeled possible causes and verification methods. Candidate signals alone cannot establish waste, faults, poor quality, or guaranteed savings, alter existing user decisions, or apply changes automatically. Cache shares use compatible observed input/cache-read pairs; unknown denominators do not produce ratios.

G11: provide input-growth, uncached-input-jump, and before/after-compaction trajectories using reliable turn or request order, with explicit boundary objects, analysis windows, and methods. Recorded input usage is not context occupancy; pressure ratios require reliable context size and matching capacity evidence. Without capacity, retain observed input changes without asserting proximity to a limit. Compaction epochs, model changes, missing measurements, and uncertain order create explicit boundaries. Do not produce undisclosed cross-boundary trends, attribute changes to adjacent tools, or establish benefits from splitting tasks solely from growth.

G12: aggregate reads, write intent, tests, and calculable duration by reliable resource identity, separating terminal results from independently observed changes. Resource keys retain source/project evidence; path substrings do not establish projects, and same-name files across projects or unresolved targets must not merge. Repeated reads never reduce the ledger, and operations receive no Token or cost allocation. Overlapping duration for one object uses a disclosed union or sum basis. Hotspots describe activity distribution; timing and coverage are needed for further bottleneck investigation. This does not replace G06 cleanup candidates.

G13: provide a one-query period review that assembles totals, concentration, contributions, leading tasks, model/tool distributions, and supported investigation features from one view, timezone, scope, and explicit week start. Reuse delivered queries and G10–G12. Full-scope summaries cannot come from the current page, and overlapping families cannot be added together. Disclose ongoing periods, unpriced usage, and unavailable sections independently without discarding other valid facts. Web, CLI, and Skill consume the same structured result; Codex writes the synthesis. Do not restore HTML reports. The exclusion of continuous monitoring is partially superseded by [host-driven monitoring](../../implemented/product/2026-10-09-task-statistics-monitoring.en.md); installed background scheduling remains excluded.

G14: retain native allowance observations obtained through authorized reads and determine adjacent-observation compatibility from account identity, bucket, window duration, and reset cycle. Account changes, resets, expiration, missingness, and incompatible boundaries cannot form consumption intervals. Current values cannot fill historical gaps. Concurrent local activity supplies facts from a fixed time window; it cannot attribute allowance movement to a project, task, or model, derive remaining subscription allowance from local Tokens, or predict exhaustion. Ordinary queries read stored observations. This scope does not authorize background polling, notifications, or allowance resets.

### Shared evidence routes and Agent acceptance

G15: candidates, comparisons, trajectories, hotspots, and reviews use shared typed evidence references containing logical identity, query scope, view, and analysis-method version, with direct task/turn/operation/resource navigation. Keep display labels separate from locating identities. Rebuilding identical sources preserves valid logical identities; page ordinals and database row IDs cannot locate evidence. Semantic splits or merges after fact correction cannot impersonate the original object. Expired fixed views fail explicitly; reacquire the original scope and check complete identities rather than retaining stale ranks. Source coordinates establish provenance without returning or storing raw conversation/tool bodies. Continue supporting current formats only, without old-version migrations or compatibility layers.

G16: establish repeatable Agent-query acceptance against the installed Skill and actual CLI/Web entries. Use isolated synthetic sources and independent truth; record host, model, build, cold/warm state, time to first useful result, calls/retries, response bytes, and model Tokens. Fixed questions cover named-query routing, follow-up evidence, missing versus zero, estimates versus facts, partial coverage, expiration, and unsupported conclusions. Answers cannot use raw logs, direct databases, or other side channels. Measure Wombat baselines before setting call and resource budgets. Automated acceptance does not replace G09 human usefulness judgment or run extra models to establish optimization benefits.

Delivery order is G10, G11, and their minimum G15 evidence flow first; G12 and G13 next; then G14, which needs independent account observations. G16 applies to every batch, and G01–G09 retain their existing scope. The [live-index proposal](../architecture/2026-09-30-live-usage.en.md) continues to own query and temporary-storage scale. Paginated output does not establish bounded computation; new queries cannot justify default full-event copies or projections without consumers.

The current batch connects implemented investigations, trajectories, hotspots, reviews, and configuration checks through the [Skill flow](2026-10-04-codex-skill.en.md), with query routing, processing in the current conversation, and verification suited to the question. Preserve separate evidence requirements and later acceptance for complete G01 events, G04 repeated injection, G05–G06 continuous inactivity and cleanup, and G08 historical adoption. Those requirements do not block presentation and advice based on other existing observations. Users see findings, evidence, and next steps. Check identity, versions, and coverage remain in the underlying model and expand on demand. Reuse existing candidates, configuration recommendations, user decisions, and evidence types; combining answers does not require a second domain model.

### Check architecture and rule methods from COT

Adopt COT's shared statistical inputs, pure check functions, centrally defined rule parameters, and concrete finding summaries to refine G10–G13. G01 continues to own event evidence for collaboration friction. Refer to the fixed-version [check snapshot](https://github.com/cot-intelligence/cot/blob/6b51be8943371d20c45d71bff9cf13ebd3461605/backend/app/insight_snapshot.py), [rule implementation](https://github.com/cot-intelligence/cot/blob/6b51be8943371d20c45d71bff9cf13ebd3461605/backend/app/insights.py), and [activity anomaly comparisons](https://github.com/cot-intelligence/cot/blob/6b51be8943371d20c45d71bff9cf13ebd3461605/backend/app/activity.py). This section defines the full incremental scope. The [core reference](../../../reference/core.en.md) owns delivered behavior and current limits. Source references do not establish delivery of every corresponding capability in Wombat. Validate thresholds with independent Codex truth rather than adopting COT's numeric values unchanged.

Keep the data flow as safe collection → canonical facts → a fixed snapshot for the selected scope → shared statistical slices → deterministic checks → existing candidates or recommendations → Skill interpretation and Web evidence. Reuse current Rust queries, activity check functions, and the configuration rule directory. Within one query, aggregate the required operations, outcomes, resources, and durations for related checks to share. Check functions receive explicit inputs, parameters, and an observation cutoff. They do not access databases, read raw sources, obtain the current time, or call models themselves. Pass only bounded statistics and evidence references needed for the query. The live-index proposal owns computation and memory budgets; do not copy COT's approach of retaining the complete event set in memory.

Each owning Rust module centrally declares stable rule identity, scope, required evidence, and method and parameter versions. Separate statistical calculation from evaluation. Web and Skill consume generated contracts without separate thresholds or failure-rate calculations. Failure rates use operations with determinate terminal outcomes as the denominator. Combine start/end records into one operation, deduplicate replays, treat expected search misses separately from failures, and preserve independent parent/child operations. Each check returns its facts and gaps independently. One failed check does not block checks without dependencies on it. Reuse existing result types and extend Rust DTOs only for new statistics with actual consumers, without a general rule language or a second domain store.

Organize activity summaries by existing operation categories, reliable resource identities, and leading targets, with direct evidence navigation. Finding summaries use object, window, measured value/comparison, and next step. Combine multiple signals about the same problem in presentation without deleting underlying check facts. Show a small set of locatable, actionable problems first, while retaining other results and full-scope totals. Usage candidates remain query results. Configuration recommendations, user decisions, and rechecks retain their separate meanings. A shared summary does not require persistent problem entities or COT's automatic resolution after several days without a match. Provide advice immediately when partial facts support it. Codex states possible causes explicitly and supplies a verification action.

Canonical operations now deliver repeated slow requests, historical failure and duration comparisons, identical requests across tasks, and repeatedly failed or declined requests within a task. The [core reference](../../../reference/core.en.md) owns current behavior and budgets. Unanswered questions and complete approval exchanges still require safe host events; callable names or missing results cannot establish them. Independent synthetic truth covers both sides of thresholds, minimum-sample boundaries, insufficient history, current-window outliers excluded from the baseline, zero/unknown denominators, duplicates and out-of-order records, same-name targets across projects, expected nonzero exits, partial failures, and replay with a fixed time. Verify shared aggregation for computation budgets and consistent results separately. Actual CLI, Web, and Skill paths follow G15–G16 acceptance; pure-function tests do not replace entry-point usefulness checks.

## Alternatives considered

Static size, read counts, or absent events cannot justify waste, inactivity, or savings, so retain unknown states and implement evidence adapters first. Demonstrations or test counts cannot replace fault and interaction journeys. Native handoff supersedes Wombat-owned generation, application, execution receipts, and recovery; acceptance work must not restore them.

An MCP wrapper for the Skill would duplicate lifecycle and contracts. Reconsider only for multi-host tool discovery or measured CLI overhead, not as an assumed performance improvement.

Ranking totals alone does not provide a verifiable investigation route, while treating rule matches as diagnoses exceeds the evidence. Use bounded fact candidates with Codex interpretation, reusing Rust queries and native handoff.

## Acceptance criteria

G01–G09 remain agreed full-product scope, delivered and accepted separately by capability. They are not shared prerequisites for every common-question batch, and partial delivery does not complete them all. Independent truth must cover thresholds, failure, retries, parent/child replay, different versions, and unknowns. Web/CLI share facts and per-rule failures must not block independent checks. The [bounded-rule](../../implemented/architecture/2026-10-02-startup-static-rules.en.md) and [review-integrity](../../implemented/architecture/2026-10-02-rule-review-integrity.en.md) decisions own static-block, declared-copy, and Hook-reference regression rationale.

Deliver G10–G16 in the stated order. Each batch requires generated contracts, Web/noninteractive entries, a Skill query route, and independent truth. Candidate tests cover no matches, zero/unknown denominators, low coverage, conflicting signals, and dominant outliers. Trajectories cover constant/growing inputs, isolated outliers, missing measurements, compaction, model changes, tied times, and late records. Check predefined facts and metrics directly rather than saving production answers as expected results.

Resource hotspots cover same-name paths across projects, aliases, unresolved targets, successful writes without observed changes, overlapping duration, and parent/child replay. Reviews cover date boundaries, daylight saving, ongoing periods, paginated contribution conservation, overlapping families, and independently degraded sections. Allowance history covers repeated observations, account changes, reset cycles, expiration, missingness, and incompatible intervals without reconstructing account consumption from local usage.

Evidence navigation requires actual browser/CLI checks for one view, retained filters, complete identities, cancellation, refresh, rebuilding, expiration, and unknown-version rejection, while preserving user decisions and handling records. Agent acceptance retains actual call, byte, Token, and latency evidence, assessing accuracy and resource budgets separately. API, installation, or synthetic-transport success cannot establish natural-language routing or human-task outcomes. Disclose untested platforms, models, and long-history scopes.

Full acceptance failed in the 2026-10-04 [product evidence](../../../benchmarks/2026-10-04-product-acceptance-feedback.json); subsequent [repair evidence](../../../benchmarks/2026-10-04-product-acceptance-fixes.json) does not replace product reacceptance. F01 needs sending-specific errors, installation recovery, and no automatic retry of unknown delivery. F02 needs correct instruction/extension scope and estimated-token copy. F03 needs task purpose, filtered summaries, reliable turns/suggestion associations, on-demand identity details, and recent/all/highest-usage navigation, with full visual acceptance outstanding. Those artifacts alone retain build and scenario outcomes, without another status table.

Verify assembled entries for single and complete batch selection, shared files, per-project cwd, content/evidence-change rejection, disconnects after acceptance, and rechecks. Request acceptance is not resolution; retain neither historical send deduplication nor Wombat execution receipts. Builds and prototypes cannot replace actual interfaces or human-task outcomes.

## Risks

Sources/hosts without complete events and coverage may leave capabilities unknown; never lower evidence thresholds to manufacture completion. Accounts, runtime observations, platforms, and long runs require different conditions; focused success does not generalize. Resource targets remain pending; prioritizing functionality does not cancel performance requirements.
